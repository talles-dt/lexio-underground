## Resultado da situação de uso

The listing claims soundproofing, but the short quiet visit does not establish it. The room appears well-maintained; no inspection report is available. Two users found it cramped for six people with equipment, not for every possible use. They reported disruption during morning works; effects on non-respondents are unknown. Pedir condições de uso e evidência pertinente, sem emitir certificação.
