## Resultado da situação de uso

A slight delay understates the three-hour wait. The bag hints at departure, but no ticket establishes a settled plan. The remark may suggest ambivalence about leaving. We should avoid reading a definite decision into the smile. A possible subtext is attachment alongside desire for change. Evocative describes a reader’s associations, not an identical response from everyone.
