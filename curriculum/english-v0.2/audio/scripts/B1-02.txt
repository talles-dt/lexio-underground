Interviewer: Had the caretaker left before you arrived?
Sam: Yes. We arrived at six, and he had left at five forty-five.
Interviewer: Could you contact him?
Sam: At first, no. Then Mina found the number, and we managed to reach him at six twenty.
Interviewer: Did your group use to collect a key every Friday?
Sam: No. We used to have our own key. We would arrive early and set up the chairs.
Interviewer: Who failed to pass on the message?
Sam: I can’t identify that from what I know.
