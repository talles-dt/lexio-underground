Interviewer: Did you use to sing alone, Mara?
Mara: No, I didn’t. I used to sing with a choir on Fridays.
Interviewer: Do you still sing on Fridays?
Mara: No. Now I sing with a group on Sundays.
Interviewer: Did the rain stop the performance?
Mara: No. Although it rained, we performed indoors.
Interviewer: What happened after the songs?
Mara: We talked to the audience and put the chairs away.
