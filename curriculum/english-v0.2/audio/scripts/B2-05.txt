Visitor: Can I borrow a camera from the upper shelf?
Volunteer: Those cameras are awaiting inspection. They aren’t available yet.
Visitor: What about the recorders stored in the blue cabinet?
Volunteer: They’re available for a two-day loan.
Visitor: If I return one at four thirty, can someone else borrow it immediately?
Volunteer: No. We record the return that day, but inspect it the next morning.
Visitor: So recording a return is not the same as approving the item?
Volunteer: Exactly. Staff carry out the inspection before changing its availability.
